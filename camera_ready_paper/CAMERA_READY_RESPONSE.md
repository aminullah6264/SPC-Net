## Update 2026-11-11: comparative-study framing

- Paper reframed as **model + controlled comparative study** (abstract, Sec. 1 contributions, Secs. 3, 4.4–4.6, 5).
- Single-prompt baseline (simple decoder, BCE). Ours = + prompt bank (max) + adaptive fallback: +0.2 / +0.3 S_m points on DUTS-TE / DUT-OMRON (3 seeds), on par elsewhere. Neither component helps on its own.
- **Point loss removed** from the method; reported as a studied alternative (it lowers S_m on all six benchmarks).
- Residual, attention aggregation, multi-token VLF (VLF-K, 3 seeds), BiSAM and the correlation-aware decoder are studied alternatives with no consistent gain.
- Fine-tuned vs frozen encoders: +0.8 to +4.4 points.
- New tables: Tab. 3 (prompt side) and Tab. 4 (architecture/training), replacing the earlier Tab. 3/4. Residual is now Eq. 4.
- Framework figure: the caption says residual/attention are not part of the final model. **The residual arrow and star must still be removed in `SOD_framework.pptx`.**
- `rebuttal_camera_ready.tex` (1 page) is the LaTeX version of this response.
